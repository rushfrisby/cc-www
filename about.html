Who We Are

About Cleverly Contrived

We started this company because we believe custom doesn’t have to mean complicated — it just means it was built with intention.

Cleverly Contrived was founded on a simple observation: most software isn’t built for the people who use it. It’s built for the average case, the common denominator, the broadest possible market. That works for some. But for businesses with specialized operations, unique workflows, or problems that don’t fit neatly into existing categories — “good enough” isn’t good enough.

We started this company to be the team you call when off-the-shelf falls short. When you need hardware that doesn’t exist yet, firmware that bridges the gap between physical and digital, software that mirrors your exact process, or integrations that connect it all together.

We’re not a body shop, and we’re not a template factory. We’re engineers and designers who take the time to understand your problem before we start solving it. The result is technology that fits your business like it was always supposed to be there — because, in a way, it was. We just hadn’t built it yet.

How We Work

Our Approach

We don’t start with technology. We start with your problem.

Understand

Deep discovery to learn your domain, your constraints, and your goals. We become students of your business before we write a line of code.

Design

Architecture and planning that accounts for today’s needs and tomorrow’s growth. Every decision documented, every trade-off discussed.

Build

Iterative development with continuous feedback. You see working software early and often — not just at the end.

Deliver

Thorough testing, careful deployment, and comprehensive documentation. We hand you a system you can understand and maintain.

What Drives Us

What We Believe

Precision Over Speed

Rushing creates debt. We take the time to do it right the first time, so you’re not paying to fix it later. Thoughtful engineering isn’t slow — it’s efficient.

Understanding Over Assumptions

We’d rather ask a hundred questions than guess once. The better we understand your world, the better we can build for it. Every great solution starts with listening.

Ownership Over Handoffs

We don’t throw code over a wall. We stand behind what we build, support what we deliver, and treat your success as our own.
